About Our Institution

Academic Legacy & Location

Located in the scenic landscape of Drugyel, Paro, Lorig Jungney Training Institute serves as a trusted center for technical training and academic growth. Founded last year, we bridge the gap between traditional learning and modern technological skills.

Lorig Jungney Training Institute is a dedicated coaching center that helps students excel in the RCSC and Preliminary Examination through expert teaching, focused subject training, regular mock tests, and a supportive learning environment that builds confidence and ensures success.

We provide students with rigorous training programs designed to prepare them directly for corporate operations, technology management, and higher academic success.

VISION

To be a trusted and learning institute in Bhutan that empowers learners to succeed through equity, equitable, and outcome-oriented academic coaching.

MISSION

To prove structured, affordable, and learner-centered academic support that prepares students for national competitive examinations through qualified faculty, rigorous assessment, and a commitment to excellence and integrity in education.
